Rules in 1.1

Focus ring
3px solid outline, 2px offset. The colour is --ys-focus-color. Without it, the ring has the text colour. Press Tab on this page to see the real rule.
Links
Underline offset 0.2em, thickness 1px (0.0625em on large text). The bundle does not add or remove an underline.
Reduced motion
Smooth scroll stops. The identity has no animation of its own. A screenshot cannot show this rule.
Print
White paper, black text, also from a dark page. The address prints after a body-text link. The nav element and decorative images do not print. See the PDF.
Footer line
The class ys-footer-line: small, muted, the link has the colour of the line and an underline.
